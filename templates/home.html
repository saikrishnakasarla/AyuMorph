How AyuMorph Works

A streamlined pipeline from leaf photograph to verified classification

1. Image Acquisition

Upload or drop a high-resolution photograph of the plant specimen (leaf, flower, or stem). The server validates format, dimensions, and image integrity.

2. MobileNetV2 Feature Extraction

Image is resized to 224×224 and normalized. The deep convolutional MobileNetV2 network extracts hierarchical morphological features with transfer learning.

3. Classification & Insights

Computes prediction confidence and pairs the classified specimen with verified botanical records, morphology markers, and documented traditional uses.
