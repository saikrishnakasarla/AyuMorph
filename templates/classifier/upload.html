Specimen Capture Guidelines
  • Isolated Subject: Focus closely on a clean leaf or flower cluster without human hands or clutter in the frame.
  • Uniform Lighting: Capture images under natural daylight to prevent deep shadows or lens glare.
  • Sharp Focus: Ensure venation, leaf margins, and textures are sharp for maximum feature extraction fidelity.
  • Standard Orientation: Center the leaf vertically or horizontally on a neutral background where feasible.
